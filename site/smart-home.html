Smart devices are only as good as the wiring behind them. Many smart switches need a neutral wire that older Boston homes don't always have in the switch box. We figure out what will work in your house, install it properly, and make sure you know how to use it before we leave.

Smart Home Automation FAQ

My smart switch needs a neutral wire. What if I don't have one?

Some older boxes don't. We can run a neutral where practical, or recommend no-neutral devices that work with your setup.

Do you supply the devices?

You can buy your own and we'll install them, or we can recommend devices that work well together.

Can you install a smart thermostat?

Yes, for most common systems. We'll confirm your heating and cooling wiring is compatible first.
