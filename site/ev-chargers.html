EV Charger Installation in Dorchester & Boston

A Level 2 charger turns overnight charging from a trickle into a full battery by morning. The charger itself is the easy part. What matters is whether your panel can carry the load, how far the wire has to run to your driveway or garage, and that it's installed to code. We check all three up front and give you a straight answer.

When homeowners call us for this

  • You just bought or are about to buy an EV or plug-in hybrid
  • Charging from a regular wall outlet is too slow for your commute
  • You have a charger in the box and need it installed properly
  • You want an outdoor charger at a driveway, garage or parking spot behind a triple-decker

What's involved

  1. Panel and load check

    We look at your panel's size and how much load it already carries. If there's capacity, we add a dedicated 240-volt circuit. If not, we'll explain your options, from a load-managing charger to a panel upgrade, and what each costs.

  2. Choosing the location

    We plan the shortest safe route from the panel to where you park, and talk through hardwired vs. plug-in (NEMA 14-50) setups and outdoor-rated equipment.

  3. Installation

    We run the circuit, mount and connect the charger, and set it to the correct amperage for the circuit. Wiring is secured and protected, not left draped along the wall.

  4. Permit, test and handoff

    We file the permit, test the charger with your car if it's home, and show you how to use it.

EV Charger Installation FAQ

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. Many homes have enough capacity for a dedicated circuit. We check your panel's load first. If it's tight, a load-managing charger can sometimes avoid a full upgrade. We'll show you both options.

Should I buy the charger, or do you supply it?

Either works. If you already have one, we'll install it. If you haven't bought one yet, call first and we'll tell you what amperage your home can support, so you don't buy the wrong unit.

Can you install a charger at a triple-decker or condo?

Often, yes. The main questions are which meter and panel serve your parking spot and how far the run is. Condo installs may also need association approval. We'll help you figure out what's needed.

How long does installation take?

A simple install close to the panel is often done in a few hours to a day. Longer runs or a panel upgrade add time, and we'll tell you up front.

Ask us about financing options when we quote, especially if the job includes a panel upgrade.
