Outlets & Switches FAQ

Do you take small jobs like one outlet?

Yes. Customers tell us they appreciate that we treat small jobs with the same care as big ones.

Can I just swap a two-prong outlet for a three-prong one?

Not without a ground. Putting a three-prong outlet on an ungrounded circuit is a code violation and a safety risk. We can add a ground or install GFCI protection labeled correctly.

Why does my outlet feel warm?

Warm outlets usually mean a loose connection or an overloaded circuit. Stop using it and call us. It's worth checking soon.
