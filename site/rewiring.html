Home Rewiring in Dorchester & Boston

Much of Boston's housing was built long before modern electrical loads. Knob-and-tube, cloth-insulated wire and ungrounded two-prong outlets are common in older Dorchester and South Boston homes. Rewiring is a bigger job, so we lay out the plan, the phases and the price clearly before anyone touches a wall.

When homeowners call us for this

  • Knob-and-tube or cloth-covered wiring in the basement or attic
  • Mostly two-prong outlets with no ground
  • Flickering lights, warm outlets or switch plates, or a burning smell
  • An insurer or inspector has flagged your wiring
  • You're renovating and walls are already open

What's involved

  1. Inspection and plan

    We trace what's there, identify what needs replacing now and what can wait, and give you a written plan. Partial rewiring is often an option.

  2. Phased work

    We work room by room where possible so you can keep living in the house. We fish new wire through existing walls to limit openings, and tell you up front where openings are needed.

  3. Permit and inspection

    Rewiring is permitted work. We file it and schedule rough and final inspections as required.

  4. Wrap-up

    New outlets, switches and grounding in place, a labeled panel, and a clean house.

Home Rewiring FAQ

Do I have to move out during a rewire?

Usually not. We work in phases so most of the house stays powered. We'll plan around your schedule.

How much wall damage will there be?

We fish wire through existing cavities wherever possible. Some openings are usually needed, and we'll show you where before starting and agree up front on patching.

Is knob-and-tube wiring dangerous?

It was installed well for its time, but much of it is now 80 to 100 years old, ungrounded, and often has been modified or buried in insulation. Many insurers won't cover it. We'll assess yours and tell you honestly how urgent it is.

Can I rewire in stages?

Yes. Many homeowners start with the most-used rooms or the riskiest circuits. We'll help you prioritize.

Is financing available?

Ask us about financing options when we quote. For larger rewires it can make sense to phase the work too.
