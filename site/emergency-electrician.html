Emergency Electrical Service FAQ

What should I do right now?

If there's smoke, fire or a strong burning smell, get everyone out and call 911. If it's safe to do so, turn off the breaker for the affected circuit. Then call us.

A power line is down outside. Who do I call?

Stay well away from it, keep others away, and call 911 and Eversource right away. Once the utility has made it safe, we can repair damage to your service mast, meter socket or panel.

Half my house has no power. Is that an emergency?

Often, yes. Partial power loss can mean a failing main breaker or a problem with the service connection. Call us and we'll help you figure out if it's urgent.

Should I use the form for emergencies?

No. Please call. The form is for non-urgent jobs.
