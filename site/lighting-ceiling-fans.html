Lighting & Ceiling Fans FAQ

Can you add recessed lights without opening up the whole ceiling?

In many cases, yes. We fish wire between fixture openings. Access depends on joists and what's above, and we'll tell you before we start if any extra openings are needed.

Can a ceiling fan go where my light fixture is now?

Usually, but the box has to be rated for a fan. Many older boxes aren't. We swap it for a fan-rated box so the fan is secure and doesn't wobble.

Do you supply the fixtures?

You can buy your own and we'll install them, or we can recommend fixtures that fit the space and your budget.
